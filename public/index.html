Vision

We build when
something needs
to exist.

Not for metrics. Not to fill a portfolio. We build when a problem is real and no one has solved it well enough.

01
We use what we build
Every product we ship starts from a real problem we personally experience. If we wouldn't use it daily, we don't build it.
02
Depth over volume
We'd rather spend a year making one great product than three months shipping four forgettable ones. The market doesn't need more noise.
03
Privacy is fundamental
We design every product with a privacy-first architecture. User data stays on-device wherever possible. No surveillance, no data resale — ever.
04
Long-term commitment
We maintain and improve everything we ship. Launching is the beginning, not the end. We're building things meant to last.
