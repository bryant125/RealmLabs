How it works

Built for one thumb and zero spare brain cells.

Tap to log. Glance to understand. MamaBee does the remembering so you can do the cuddling.

Effortless logging

Big, thumb-friendly buttons in the bottom of the screen. Sleep, feeds, diapers, meds — one tap, with a gentle haptic to confirm.

Good questions

Everything you might be wondering.

Is MamaBee free? +
Yes — all core logging, insights, sync and export are free, forever. A Premium tier with the gentle AI features is coming soon.
Does my baby's data train AI models? +
No. Ever. Your logs live on your device and sync only through your private iCloud. When you use MamaBee Chat, only a short, anonymised summary goes to Google's Gemini — never your baby's name or photo, and never to train AI models.
Can I share with my partner, nanny or grandma? +
Yes — co-parent sync is free for up to 2 caregivers. Premium unlocks unlimited caregiver sharing.
What happens if I cancel Premium? +
All your data stays exactly where it is — you keep everything you've logged. Only the AI features turn off.
Why iPhone only? +
We're iOS-first. Android, iPad and Apple Watch are on the roadmap — but only when we can do them genuinely well.
What languages do you support? +
12 at launch: English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Русский, ไทย and Tiếng Việt.
Can I export my data? +
Yes — CSV, a PDF pediatrician report, and JSON. Free, anytime, with no lock-in.
Does it work offline? +
Yes — all core logging works offline. Sync happens quietly the moment you're connected again.
