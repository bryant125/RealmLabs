What MamaBee is — and what it isn't

  • It is NOT: medical advice, a diagnostic tool, or a substitute for a paediatrician. If your baby is unwell, call a real doctor. AI insights, sleep predictions, growth percentiles, and milestone guidance are all informational only.

MamaBee AI Assistant

The AI Assistant is a convenience feature powered by a third-party model (Google's Gemini Flash). When you use it:

  • It can be wrong. AI hallucinates. Treat its answers as a starting point, not a final word.
  • We only send the data described in the Privacy Policy ("MamaBee AI Assistant"). Never your baby's name, photo, or free-text notes.
  • Don't use the assistant to get a medical diagnosis. It will refuse, and you shouldn't trust it if it doesn't.
