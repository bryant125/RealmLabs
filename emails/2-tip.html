The #1 sleep mistake we made

Quick one today.

For weeks, our biggest mistake was watching the clock instead of the baby. We'd force naps at “nap o'clock” and end up with an overtired baby who fought sleep even harder.

The fix that changed everything — wake windows + sleepy cues:

  • Time sleep by how long your baby's been awake (chart's in your cheat-sheet)
  • Watch the early cues — yawning, red eyebrows, the faraway stare
  • Start winding down before the fussing (fussing is the late signal)

Overtired babies don't sleep better — they sleep worse. Catch the window and everything gets easier.
